# Privacy Policy for Money Goal

**Last updated: 14 October 2026**

Money Goal ("the app") is a savings goal tracker for Android. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Summary

Money Goal does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

## Information the app stores

The app stores the following only in a local database on your device:

- Goal names, target amounts, target dates, and currencies
- Contributions you log toward your goals
- App preferences, such as default currency and dark mode

This information is never sent to me or to any third party. I have no access to it.

## Information the app does not collect

- No personal information (name, email address, phone number)
- No account or sign-in information
- No location data
- No device identifiers or advertising IDs
- No analytics, crash reports, or usage tracking
- No financial account or payment information. The app only records amounts you type in yourself, and it does not connect to any bank or payment service.

## Advertising and third parties

The app contains no ads and uses no third-party analytics or advertising services.

## Permissions

The app declares network permissions that are included by default in the framework it is built with. The app does not use them to send your data anywhere.

## Backups

If Android's Auto Backup is enabled on your device, your app data may be backed up to your own Google account by Android. This is controlled by your device settings and Google's policies, not by the app or its developer.

## Data deletion

You can delete your data at any time by deleting individual goals within the app, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it in Android settings.

## Children's privacy

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
